## 1. What do champions/finalists and advisors say about strategy (pricing, inventory, marketing, staffing, cash, timing, run-to-run experimentation)?

### Takeaway
Public, citable strategy advice for the Entrepreneurship track specifically is thin. Most of the available advice is generic Knowledge Matters (KM) hint content from 2017-18 (staffing to traffic, product placement, shrinkage/security, hiring quality) plus official descriptions of which levers exist (financing, team, resources, pricing, marketing). Winners' own write-ups stress effort and iteration volume more than specific parameter settings.

### Cited Findings
**Event scope and levers (official, current):**
- VBC Entrepreneurship: participants "conduct market research, spot new business opportunities and ... embark on their own entrepreneurial venture with 20 different businesses to choose from." They implement strategy by "requesting financing, building their team, acquiring resources, setting prices, developing their marketing and more," while "analyzing market data, interpreting financial reports." — [DECA: VBC-Entrepreneurship](https://www.deca.org/compete/virtual-business-challenge-entrepreneurship); [DECA+](https://www.decaplus.org/competitive-events/virtual-business-challenge-entrepreneurship)
- DECA frames all VBCs as "efficiently maintain a virtual business while achieving high profits and customer satisfaction." — [DECA Direct: Beginner's Guide to the VBCs](https://www.decadirect.org/articles/beginners-guide-to-the-deca-virtual-business-challenges) (snippet; date not visible)
- In the KM Entrepreneurship sim, students "start with one business and one location, and work to grow and expand ... to multiple businesses and multiple locations"; the 4 Ps (product, price, place, promotion) are the core decision frame. — [Knowledge Matters: Entrepreneurship](https://www.knowledgematters.com/high-school/entrepreneurship/); [KM DECA page](https://www.knowledgematters.com/high-school/competitions/deca/)
- The classroom version of the sim covers market research, an elevator pitch, a business plan and raising money. Projects are named "Shark Project, Business Plan Project, and Mega-Mogul Project." — [Knowledge Matters: Entrepreneurship](https://www.knowledgematters.com/high-school/entrepreneurship/); [eDynamic Learning VB Entrepreneurship course](https://www.edynamiclearning.com/course/virtual-business-simulation-entrepreneurship/) (the course page is a reseller, not a primary source)

**KM official hints (OLDER: 2017-2018, retail-style sims; they transfer to Entrepreneurship's staffing, layout and inventory mechanics):**
- Staffing: "Observe your store to see when the busier and slower customer traffic times are throughout the day and staff your store accordingly." Some customers will not buy certain products without a salesperson's help. — [KM: VBC Hints 2017-2018](https://www.knowledgematters.com/blog/virtual-business-challenge-hints-2017-2018/)
- Layout and inventory: the default floor layout "is not necessarily the ideal floor layout"; "You control traffic flow with the way you position your products"; "Stockers replenish the goods on the shelves. Customers will not purchase what they cannot find." — [KM: VBC Hints 2017-2018](https://www.knowledgematters.com/blog/virtual-business-challenge-hints-2017-2018/)
- Shrinkage: the file starts with no security. "Keep an eye on your inventory report so that you may monitor shrinkage"; security options reduce it. — [KM: VBC Hints 2017-2018](https://www.knowledgematters.com/blog/virtual-business-challenge-hints-2017-2018/)
- Hiring: "the best job candidates will be ones with no typos in their resumes and the ones who have good references." — [KM: FBLA-PBL VBC Hints 2017-2018](https://www.knowledgematters.com/blog/fbla-pbl-virtual-business-challenge-hints-2017-2018/)
- Use the **Week-Part Sales report** to see customer traffic during the week and set staffing. — surfaced in a search summary tied to [KM: VBC Hints 2017-2018](https://www.knowledgematters.com/blog/virtual-business-challenge-hints-2017-2018/) *(attribution to that exact page is uncertain)*
- KM also posted per-track hints on Facebook ("VBC Challenge Hints!! ... VBC Accounting/Pricing ..."), and a [FCCLA hints](https://www.knowledgematters.com/blog/fccla-virtual-business-challenge-hints-2017-2018/) variant exists. — [KM Facebook post](https://www.facebook.com/KnoMatters/posts/vbc-challenge-hintshere-are-virtual-business-challenge-hintsvbc-accountingpricin/1999841876906175/)

**Adjacent track (Personal Finance), older student guide:**
- Bruce Hong's guide: PF score is "determined purely by the simulated character's net worth at the end of the two years," so "profitability and change in net worth should be given the highest priority in decision-making." A practical first step is opening a checking account, which is needed to pay bills. — [Bruce Hong, DECA VBC Personal Finance (deca.ca PDF)](http://deca.ca/documents/Masters./VBCPF.pdf) (undated; likely pre-2022)

**Winner write-ups (2024, Restaurant track, anecdotal):**
- Jerry Huang, VBC Restaurant (VBCRS): reports **1st place internationally in qualifying Round 1**, beating second place in his region "by 8%." Team spent **~80 hours over the 10-day round**. Tips: "study ahead" (he finished two weeks of math homework early to free up time); "relentless hard work will always beat talent"; many teams "quit before even getting top 10 in their region." Published Oct 27, 2024. — [Jerry Huang, Medium](https://medium.com/@jerryhuang1128/from-zero-to-first-place-my-strategy-for-winning-decas-vbc-qualifying-round-29a2125688f1) (only snippets retrieved; the specific in-sim tactics in the article were NOT retrievable)
- Eric Yang (DECA/FBLA VBC advocate): "If you're not willing to put in a good few hours everyday for the 10-day qualification period, you're likely better off spending that time preparing for a written or roleplay"; teams spend "upwards of 80 hours in 10 days to qualify for ICDC." — [Eric Yang, Medium](https://medium.com/@eric8yang/the-ins-and-out-s-of-the-deca-fbla-virtual-business-challenge-9928c227eddd) (date not visible)

**Generic sim-economics warning:**
- "Mismanaging cash flow can lead to bankruptcy, and overspending on marketing while neglecting production will result in angry customers with no products to buy." — surfaced in a search summary for VB Entrepreneurship-related pages *(exact source page unclear; treat as low confidence)*

### Inferences
- The levers a tracker should log per run and per week follow from the official description and hints: business type chosen (1 of 20), location, financing/loan amount, prices per product, marketing spend by channel, staff count and schedule (by week-part), wages, inventory/reorder levels, security spend, layout changes, expansion timing (new location/business), cash balance and profit.
- Since only the best score counts and runs are unlimited in qualifying, the winning approach is high-volume, controlled experimentation. That fits the 80-hour figures.

### Gaps
- No retrievable, specific Entrepreneurship-track parameter advice (e.g., optimal markup %, loan size, when to expand) from a named champion. The Huang and Yang articles probably hold more detail, but fetches were blocked.
- No r/DECA content could be retrieved (reddit blocked; search engine did not index relevant threads).
- Current (2024-26) KM official hint pages for Entrepreneurship were not found. The only KM hints surfaced are from 2017-18.

## 2. How do top teams practice (number of runs, logs/spreadsheets, A/B testing, reviewing reports, resets)?

### Takeaway
The competition structure (unlimited runs, best score counts, live leaderboard, 10-11 day windows) rewards many iterations. Winners report ~80 team-hours per round. Beyond that, no public source documents a specific logging or A/B-testing method or template.

### Cited Findings
- Qualifying rounds: two rounds, "generally ... late-October and mid-January"; during each ten-day round teams "are free to experiment with the competition simulation and have no time restrictions for each submission." — [DECA Direct: Beginner's Guide](https://www.decadirect.org/articles/beginners-guide-to-the-deca-virtual-business-challenges)
- "Students can run the sim as many times as they want. We keep track of their highest score only." — [KM: DECA VBC – DECA Advisors](https://www.knowledgematters.com/blog/deca-virtual-business-challenge-deca-advisors/); similar wording at [DECA Direct: Get Ready for Round One](https://www.decadirect.org/articles/get-ready-for-round-one-of-the-deca-virtual-business-challenge) (which says **11 days** after registration; sources differ between 10 and 11 days)
- Official guidelines: the competition sim "may be completed multiple times within the challenge period, with only the best total score being counted." Teams have **up to 3 members**. — [DECA Guide 2025-26 VBC Guidelines](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/635c470cc81318fc3e9c1e0e/68558aad1554c0d934b88d9a_HS_VBC_Guidelines.pdf); [2026-27 Guidelines](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/635c470cc81318fc3e9c1e0e/6a3c7ec48e4955853e4382d4_HS_VBC_Guidelines.pdf) (via snippet)
- A **live scoreboard** ranks every team that has completed at least one run. — [DECA Direct: Beginner's Guide](https://www.decadirect.org/articles/beginners-guide-to-the-deca-virtual-business-challenges)
- Time commitment: ~80 hours per team per 10-day round. — [Huang](https://medium.com/@jerryhuang1128/from-zero-to-first-place-my-strategy-for-winning-decas-vbc-qualifying-round-29a2125688f1); [Yang](https://medium.com/@eric8yang/the-ins-and-out-s-of-the-deca-fbla-virtual-business-challenge-9928c227eddd)
- Qualifier lists are published after each round, so they are a data source for benchmarking: [R1 2023](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/635c470cc81318fc3e9c1e0e/63b4598767e1cc0cc8a05e9e_DECA-VBC-Round-1-2023-ICDC-Qualifiers.pdf), [R2 2024](https://assets-global.website-files.com/635c470cc81318fc3e9c1e0e/65d4fbb8f6f185cac74bb6b2_DECA%20VBC%20Round%202%202024%20ICDC%20Qualifiers.pdf), [R1 2025](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/635c470cc81318fc3e9c1e0e/673caf8a420ac0362208cc59_Copy%20of%20DECA%20VBC%20Round%201%202025ICDC%20Qualifiers.pdf), [R2 2025](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/635c470cc81318fc3e9c1e0e/67b72b4020c9ab41f9fc43e0_DECA%20VBC%20Round%202%202025%20ICDC%20Qualifiers.pdf), [2025-26 R1 announcement](https://www.decadirect.org/articles/announcing-the-2025-2026-virtual-business-challenge-round-1-icdc-qualifiers)
- The classroom VB Entrepreneurship product (outside the competition) can serve as off-season practice. KM publishes lesson plans for it. — [KM VBE lesson plans](https://vbcourse.knowledgematters.com/publicDocs/generateLessonPlans/VBE1); [KM Entrepreneurship](https://www.knowledgematters.com/high-school/entrepreneurship/)
- General sim-learning research claim: failure in a business sim improves learning because participants reflect more on suboptimal results. — [Startup Wars resource page](https://www.startupwars.com/simulation-resources/what-is-an-entrepreneurship-simulation/) (vendor marketing; weak source; no study cited in the snippet)

### Inferences
- Because only the best run counts, "reset and retry" costs nothing except time. A tracker can therefore log every run with its decision set and final score/profit, compare against the current best, and suggest changing one variable at a time (controlled A/B), even though no source explicitly documents that method.
- The live leaderboard lets teams track their rank relative to the ICDC-qualifying cutoff over the round. A "gap to cutoff" metric fits.
- About 80 hours over 10 days is roughly 4 hours per person per day for a 2-person team *(arithmetic inference)*. Time-per-run and runs-per-day are worth tracking.

### Gaps
- No public spreadsheet or decision-log template for VBC was found.
- No source gives a typical run count per round, or describes a specific review routine (e.g., which reports to read after each run).

## 3. Common mistakes that tank scores

### Takeaway
Documented mistakes are mostly generic: under-committing time, quitting early, stock-outs or products customers can't find, staffing that doesn't match traffic, ignoring shrinkage, weak hires, and cash-flow mismanagement or overspending on marketing relative to supply.

### Cited Findings
- Under-investing time: without "a good few hours everyday" over the 10 days, a team is better off in another event. — [Yang](https://medium.com/@eric8yang/the-ins-and-out-s-of-the-deca-fbla-virtual-business-challenge-9928c227eddd)
- Quitting early or overconfidence: many teams "quit before even getting top 10 in their region" despite expecting that qualifying is easy. — [Huang](https://medium.com/@jerryhuang1128/from-zero-to-first-place-my-strategy-for-winning-decas-vbc-qualifying-round-29a2125688f1)
- Keeping the default layout (it is "not necessarily the ideal"), letting shelves go unstocked ("Customers will not purchase what they cannot find"), leaving security unset (shrinkage), staffing without regard to traffic, and too few salespeople for assisted-sale products. — [KM Hints 2017-18](https://www.knowledgematters.com/blog/virtual-business-challenge-hints-2017-2018/) (older)
- Hiring candidates with typo-filled résumés or weak references. — [KM FBLA Hints 2017-18](https://www.knowledgematters.com/blog/fbla-pbl-virtual-business-challenge-hints-2017-2018/) (older)
- Cash-flow mismanagement leading to bankruptcy; marketing overspend relative to production/inventory causes "angry customers with no products to buy." — search-summary text *(source page unclear; low confidence)*
- For PF: not opening a checking account early (bills can't be paid). — [Bruce Hong guide](http://deca.ca/documents/Masters./VBCPF.pdf) (older)

### Inferences
- A tracker can flag warning patterns: cash below a threshold, stock-out weeks, marketing spend growing faster than inventory/capacity, customer-satisfaction drops, and shrinkage spikes.

### Gaps
- No source ranks mistakes by score impact for the Entrepreneurship track specifically.

## 4. How do two-person teams split roles during the round and at ICDC (including presentation prep)?

### Takeaway
Teams can have 1-3 members. **VBC has no presentation or judge interview.** At ICDC, finalists run the sim live, in roughly 15-minute sessions that are scored purely on results. No source describing how duos split roles was found.

### Cited Findings
- Teams of up to three members, or individuals. — [DECA Direct: Get Ready for Round One](https://www.decadirect.org/articles/get-ready-for-round-one-of-the-deca-virtual-business-challenge); [2025-26 Guidelines](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/635c470cc81318fc3e9c1e0e/68558aad1554c0d934b88d9a_HS_VBC_Guidelines.pdf)
- ICDC: all VBC tracks compete in **two sessions** of about **15 minutes** each. Final ranking = **cumulative total profit** across both (net worth for Personal Finance). Each team controls the start, stop and speed of its sim file in single-player mode. — [DECA VBC Guidelines 2023-24/2024-25](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/635c470cc81318fc3e9c1e0e/669956fed8bb6fe96f6721b6_HS_VBC_Guidelines.pdf) (via snippet)
- The DECA Direct Beginner's Guide describes ICDC as 8 teams running simultaneously at podiums, each with a provided laptop and mouse, with the sim on a closed tab. It also describes a **double-elimination** format: the top 4 advance and the bottom 4 go to consolation, and a team needs at least 2 wins to reach the finals. A live scoreboard shows positions in real time; "15 minutes for one submission." — [DECA Direct: Beginner's Guide](https://www.decadirect.org/articles/beginners-guide-to-the-deca-virtual-business-challenges) **Conflict:** the guidelines describe two sessions with cumulative profit, while this article describes double elimination. The format may differ by year; check the current-year guidelines.
- ICDC sessions are held live in the exhibit hall. Finalists get medals, the top three get DECA glass and scholarships. There is over $20,000 in total scholarship money. — [DECA Direct: Beginner's Guide](https://www.decadirect.org/articles/beginners-guide-to-the-deca-virtual-business-challenges); [Texas DECA VBC tag page](https://www.texasdeca.org/txdecaresources/tag/Virtual+Business+Challenge)
- "No presentation or judges" was stated by a search-tool summary *(search-summary inference)*. It matches the official format above (ranked on sim profit), but I did not see explicit source text saying "no presentation."
- Prior final-round examples: Team "Oof" (Oak Harbor HS, WA) won VBC Hotel at ICDC Atlanta (the 18th year of VBC, 43,000+ students, 100+ finalist teams). — [KM: VBC Hotel Final Round (Video)](https://www.knowledgematters.com/blog/virtual-business-challenge-hotel-final-round-video/) (older, about 2019; KM also has [ICDC 2019 photos/videos](https://www.knowledgematters.com/blog/photos-and-videos-from-the-deca-virtual-business-challenge-live-competition-at-deca-icdc-2019/))

### Inferences
- A duo will likely divide work as "operator" (hands on the mouse, sets speed and pauses) and "analyst" (reads reports, calls adjustments), especially under the 15-minute ICDC clock. This is inference only; no source documents it.
- In qualifying, running parallel experiments (each member tests a different variable on their own runs) is a plausible way to use the 10 days. Also unsourced.
- Since ICDC sessions are short and timed, practice should include timed ~15-minute runs. A tracker could hold a "timed mode" and a profit-per-session record.

### Gaps
- No source on duo role division.
- The ICDC format differs across sources (two cumulative sessions vs. double elimination). Current-year detail is unconfirmed.

## 5. What do teams write down or track between attempts? Any shared templates?

### Takeaway
No public VBC-specific decision-log or spreadsheet template was found. The sim's own reports (inventory, week-part sales, financial statements) are the documented data sources, and score = profit or net worth.

### Cited Findings
- Reports named in official hints: the **inventory report** (monitor shrinkage) and the **Week-Part Sales report** (traffic for staffing). — [KM Hints 2017-18](https://www.knowledgematters.com/blog/virtual-business-challenge-hints-2017-2018/)
- Competitors must "interpret financial reports" and "analyze market data." — [DECA VBC-Entrepreneurship](https://www.deca.org/compete/virtual-business-challenge-entrepreneurship)
- Scored outcome: best total score in qualifying; cumulative total profit at ICDC. — [2025-26 Guidelines](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/635c470cc81318fc3e9c1e0e/68558aad1554c0d934b88d9a_HS_VBC_Guidelines.pdf); [2024-25 Guidelines](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/635c470cc81318fc3e9c1e0e/669956fed8bb6fe96f6721b6_HS_VBC_Guidelines.pdf)
- Customer satisfaction is named alongside profit as the goal. — [DECA Direct Beginner's Guide](https://www.decadirect.org/articles/beginners-guide-to-the-deca-virtual-business-challenges)

### Inferences (suggested tracker fields, derived from the above)
- **Per run:** date, round (practice/R1/R2/ICDC-mock), who operated, business type, strategy hypothesis, variable(s) changed from the best run, final score/profit, net worth, cash low point, whether a loan was taken, time spent, and a keep/discard verdict.
- **Per sim week:** revenue, profit, cash, customer satisfaction, units sold, stock-outs, shrinkage, marketing spend by channel, staff count and wages, prices, and notable events.
- **Round-level:** best score, leaderboard rank, estimated qualifying cutoff (from past qualifier lists), and hours logged by each teammate.

### Gaps
- No shared templates, Quizlet/Studocu sets or chapter spreadsheets surfaced in searches.

## 6. ICDC finals prep: judge Q&A, presentations, what judges look for

### Takeaway
This mostly doesn't apply to VBC. Finals are live sim runs scored on profit, with no judges or presentation found in any source. Prep should focus on timed runs and a rehearsed opening sequence.

### Cited Findings
- ICDC finals are live, timed (~15 min), and ranked on cumulative profit across two sessions (or a bracket, per DECA Direct). — [Guidelines](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/635c470cc81318fc3e9c1e0e/669956fed8bb6fe96f6721b6_HS_VBC_Guidelines.pdf); [DECA Direct Beginner's Guide](https://www.decadirect.org/articles/beginners-guide-to-the-deca-virtual-business-challenges)
- The only presentation-related DECA content that surfaced was generic advice for *other* events (e.g., ending with a call to action). It is not VBC-relevant. — [DECA Direct Beginner's Guide](https://www.decadirect.org/articles/beginners-guide-to-the-deca-virtual-business-challenges) (snippet mixed in with other content)

### Inferences
- An app should put less weight on presentation prep for VBC. It should support rehearsing a fixed opening "script" (the first several decisions) and timed full runs that mimic ICDC conditions: a provided laptop and mouse, and the sim opening in a fresh tab.

### Gaps
- Could not confirm whether the 2025-26 or 2026-27 ICDC VBC format includes any interview element. Check the current [2026-27 Guidelines PDF](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/635c470cc81318fc3e9c1e0e/6a3c7ec48e4955853e4382d4_HS_VBC_Guidelines.pdf) directly (it was blocked here).
